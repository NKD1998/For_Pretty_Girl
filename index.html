the actual message

Some people just have a way of making an ordinary Monday feel like less of a Monday.

You're one of those people for me.

I don't think I've ever said this properly out loud, so I'm saying it here, where you can't interrupt me halfway through.

Work is work. Things break, meetings run long, someone always replies-all. And then the day ends, and I know I'm going to see you, and somehow that's the part I've been looking forward to since about 11am.

I like that we have our own thing going. That the office has absolutely no idea. That we can sit somewhere with chai, you taking it far more seriously than any human being reasonably should, and lose two hours without noticing.

And the Kandivali drops. Long, completely unnecessary, and still my favourite part of the week.
